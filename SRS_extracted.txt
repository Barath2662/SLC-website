Design System & UI/UX Guidelines
Version 1.0

Design Philosophy
The website should not resemble a typical local travel or taxi website. Most competitors use cluttered layouts, bright colors, and excessive content.
Instead, Srii Lakshmi Cab should project the image of a premium, dependable travel brand.
The design language should communicate:
Trust
Elegance
Professionalism
Comfort
Reliability
The website should feel closer to the websites of premium hospitality brands than to a conventional cab booking service.

Design Inspiration
The overall experience should take inspiration from:
Mercedes-Benz
BMW
Taj Hotels
Volvo Buses
Emirates
Airbnb (clean layouts)
Apple (minimalism)
The goal is not to copy these brands but to emulate their clarity, refinement, and premium presentation.

My Final Recommendations (These Will Make Your Website Truly Stand Out)
After everything we've planned, here are the features I'd add beyond what most travel websites offer:
1. Dynamic Hero Based on Time
Morning: sunrise and fresh travel imagery.
Evening: warm city lights.
Night: premium night-drive visuals.
Greeting changes automatically ("Good Morning", "Good Evening").
2. Live Fleet Availability Indicator
Not actual booking availability, just a status like:
🟢 Available for Enquiries
🟡 High DemandThis creates urgency without showing prices or requiring inventory management.

Instead of listing destinations, show a beautiful map with clickable routes from Coimbatore.
4. "Plan My Journey" Instead of "Book Now"
Make this your signature branding across the site.
5. "Why This Vehicle?" Recommendations
Every destination page should explain why a specific vehicle is best for that journey.
6. Real Fleet Photos

Embed your Google Business reviews (or selected reviews) once you have them. Real reviews are a major trust signal.
8. WhatsApp-Centric Experience
Instead of forcing users through forms, make WhatsApp a first-class communication channel with pre-filled messages based on the page they're viewing.
